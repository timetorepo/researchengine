## 0.53.0 — a connectors marketplace, built-in integrations in one place

- **Connectors is a marketplace.** One row in the sidebar opens every app Composio supports, with search and categories. Connect an app from there. Composio's sign-in opens in a small window, and the list updates when it is done.
- **Built-in integrations moved into Connectors.** Files, Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, GitHub, Notion and Slack are under the *Built in* tab, with the step-by-step tutorial and the connection log. Settings has no Integrations tab any more, and the composer's plug menu opens Connectors.
- **The Composio key is pasted once.** It is entered in the marketplace and stays on this device, encrypted when the vault is on.
- **The sidebar no longer stacks sections on top of each other.** Connectors is a single row, so nothing overlaps the nav items or Settings on shorter windows.

Installers: `Research.Engine_0.53.0_x64-setup.exe` and `Research.Engine_0.53.0_amd64.deb`. Digests are in `downloads/INSTALLERS.sha256`.

Upgrade: nothing to migrate. Saved integration credentials, connections, bots, chats, projects and settings are kept. Find the integrations under Connectors, not Settings.
