## 1.1.0 — Animated backgrounds, a session switcher, built-in services

- **Session switcher.** Recent sessions open as a card deck from the top bar, or with your switcher key (Alt+Shift by default). Pick a card to go back to that session.
- **Fourteen ReactBits backgrounds.** Silk, Galaxy, Prism, Plasma and ten more are in Settings → Appearance. The background sits behind chats and home, and it switches to a static gradient in eco mode or with reduced motion.
- **Chats kind switch.** The Chats page filters by kind with an animated switch.
- **Connected tab follows your accounts.** Connectors lists the services you have connected, lets you disconnect them, and updates when a connect or disconnect happens on Composio's website.
- **Library videos take timeline notes.** Add a note at a moment in a saved video, written by you or by the AI.
- **Built-in services.** OpenSEO, VoiceStudio, Twenty and Postiz are set up under Settings → Services. OpenWhispr supplies dictation. Postiz only creates drafts, so nothing is published or scheduled by the agent.
- **Technical handbook.** `docs/technical/` documents how the project works and every UI component.

Installers: `Research.Engine_1.1.0_x64-setup.exe` and `Research.Engine_1.1.0_amd64.deb`. Digests are in `downloads/INSTALLERS.sha256`.

Upgrade: nothing to migrate. Saved bots, chats, projects and settings are kept.
