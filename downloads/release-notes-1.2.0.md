## 1.2.0 — Liquid Glass that bends, a Marketplace, a CRM, more search

- **Liquid Glass bends the backdrop.** Settings → Appearance → Glass surface → Liquid Glass. The chatbox, the Dynamic Island and toasts refract what is behind their edges with a lens. It is off by default. Bending runs in the Chromium-based app and browsers; elsewhere the same glass is a frosted blur, and Settings says which one you have.
- **Gooey Chats switch, with a plain option.** The All / Research / Coding switch stretches like liquid by default. Turn it off in Settings → Appearance for plain tabs with the same selection.
- **Marketplace replaces Connectors.** Each app has its own page, with a Ready for coding row (GitHub, Vercel, Supabase and more), what the assistant can do once connected, and its tools as an accordion you can filter by kind. Connect works from the browser and the desktop app.
- **A CRM dashboard.** The CRM page reads your Twenty people and companies live: totals, a 14-day chart, how complete the records are, the newest records, search by name, and a quick add.
- **Two more search engines.** Firecrawl (the hosted API with a key, or your own open-source core) and Crawl4AI (your own server) join the search ladder. Automatic uses them only when they are set up.
- **Handbook chapter 15.** `docs/technical/15-search-marketplace-crm.md` covers the crawlers, the Marketplace and the CRM.

Installers: `Research.Engine_1.2.0_x64-setup.exe` and `Research.Engine_1.2.0_amd64.deb`. Digests are in `downloads/INSTALLERS.sha256`, once the release build has finished.

Upgrade: nothing to migrate. Saved bots, chats, projects and settings are kept. Liquid Glass stays off until you turn it on.
